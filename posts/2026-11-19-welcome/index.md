Welcome to the AUSPICE project blog. AUSPICE brings together researchers from the University of Oxford, the University of Reading, the UK Met Office, ECMWF and NCAR to investigate the **signal-to-noise paradox**: the puzzling finding that the real world can be more predictable than our climate models suggest.

Over the course of the project we'll use this blog to share:

- new results and the ideas behind them,
- explainers on the statistics and physics of predictability,
- news from workshops, conferences and field visits.

Stay tuned!
